EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 

Brief Description of Business 

Tech Outfitters LLC is a husband‑and‑wife digital services company providing small businesses with fast, affordable, and technically sound websites, digital content, and branding. Using a proprietary “one‑click prototype” system, we generate instant website previews for local businesses and deliver them via postcard, walk‑in visits, or email. This allows clients to see value immediately and launch a professional website within minutes. Our mission is to help small businesses modernize their online presence with secure, accurate, and easy‑to‑maintain digital solutions.

BUSINESS DESCRIPTION 

Background	 

After years in corporate healthcare tech, James and Erin saw firsthand how many small businesses struggle with outdated websites, inconsistent online listings, and expensive digital agencies. They wanted to build a business that uses their combined strengths — James’s engineering background and Erin’s project leadership — to help local businesses modernize quickly and affordably. Tech Outfitters was created to give small businesses a simple, fast, and trustworthy way to get online without the complexity or cost of traditional agencies. 

Products and Services 

Instant website prototypes delivered via QR‑coded postcards 

Website design & development 

Logo creation and digital branding 

Google Business Profile setup & optimization 

Monthly website maintenance 

SEO updates 

Content updates 

Performance‑based lead generation options 

Hourly enhancement services ($75/hr)

Risk Management 

Low conversion rates → Mitigation: A/B test postcards, improve preview quality, refine messaging. 

Scope creep → Mitigation: Clear tiered packages, written change orders, hourly enhancement pricing. 

Technical issues or downtime → Mitigation: Managed hosting, automated backups, monitoring, and SLAs.

INDUSTRY CHARACTERISTICS AND TRENDS

The outlook is strong as more small businesses modernize their digital presence.
